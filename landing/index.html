How it works

From “I'm stuck” to verified, with you in control.

  1. 01

    Ask

    Say what you want and who you are. “New sales rep — schedule the follow-up on Acme for Friday.”

  2. 02

    Approve

    H Companion shows exactly what it will change. Nothing moves until you say yes.

  3. 03

    Act

    The H agent clicks and types in your real app, on screen. A stop button is always one click away.

  4. 04

    Verify

    “The agent said so” is not enough. It reopens the record and checks the result.

    bug: “done” ≠ done → check it!
  5. 05

    Escalate

    Blocked? It stops, and support receives the goal, what was seen, what was tried and the decision needed.

Control by design

You trigger it

No background watching. It starts when you ask, and only on what you approved.

Stop, anytime

Stop blocks new actions locally and cancels the agent. Nothing restarts without a new yes.

Your screenshots, your call

Support only sees the screenshots you agreed to share. Facts, claims and guesses stay labelled.
