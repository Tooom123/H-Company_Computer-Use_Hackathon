Schedule a follow-up

Activities are follow-up tasks tied to opportunities.

  1. Open the pipeline with CRM app ‣ Sales ‣ My Pipeline.
  2. Click the opportunity card, then scroll to the Chatter and click the Activities button.
  3. Enter a short title in the Summary field.
  4. Click Schedule to save the activity.
  5. The activity now appears in the chatter.
