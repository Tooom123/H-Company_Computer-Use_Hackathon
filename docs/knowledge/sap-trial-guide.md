# SAP S/4HANA Cloud — consultation in the prepared trial

These navigation notes come from the team's manual preparation on 7 November
2026, documented in `docs/sap-trial-handoff.md`. They are not an independent
verification of an agent run. No benchmark amounts, quantities or expected
answers are included. Inspect the current record to answer a business question.

## My Home and purchase drafts

The selected trial is SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition. My Home has a personal
tile named Holo - Achats à préparer, opening Manage Purchase Orders. A personal
view Holo - Mes brouillons achats filters Editing Status to Own Draft. Drafts
belong to the signed-in user, so a different account may not see the same draft.
The trial is shared and restricts some operations on master data.

## Find the intended document

Use the user's exact reference, supplier and visible status to identify a
purchase document. Our Reference is a business reference, not necessarily the
native Purchase Order number. A draft is not an ordered purchase. Check active
filters and the editing status before declaring a document missing. Do not
choose a different record just because its name is similar.

## Read values and terms

Read the currency, item quantity and unit, Net Price, Net Order Value and
Effective Value as separate fields. Inspect payment terms and pricing details
to explain a difference; do not assume a discount was earned or a payment made.
If a condition is not visible, say that the explanation remains unverified.

## Delivery and downstream documents

Delivery Schedule belongs to an item and has dates and quantities. A purchase
order date is not the delivery date or proof of receipt. Follow-On Documents can
link later activity; the presence of a link alone does not prove a receipt or
invoice is complete. Read the linked document's status if the user asks.

## Consultation boundary

These demo requests involve navigation and reading only. Do not press Order,
Post, Delete or confirm a business action; do not edit quantities, conditions,
dates or business records. Explain what the user would check and cite visible
evidence. The current screen takes precedence over remembered values or layouts.
